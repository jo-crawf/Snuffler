Opening Snuffler the first time
===============================

Snuffler is a free little program that was never registered with Apple, so the
first time you open it your Mac will say it "could not verify" Snuffler. That
is expected, and you only have to do this once:

  1. Double-click Snuffler.dmg. A window opens with two icons in it: Snuffler
     and an Applications folder. Drag Snuffler onto the Applications folder.

  2. Open your Applications folder (in Finder, choose Go > Applications) and
     double-click Snuffler there. When the warning appears, click "Done".
     (Not "Move to Trash".)

  3. Open System Settings from the Apple menu at the top left of the screen,
     and click "Privacy & Security" in the list on the left.

  4. Scroll down to the message that says Snuffler was blocked, and click
     "Open Anyway". Enter your Mac password if it asks, then click
     "Open Anyway" once more.

From then on Snuffler opens with a double-click like any other app.

On an older Mac (before macOS 15 "Sequoia") there is a shortcut instead of
steps 3 and 4: right-click Snuffler, choose "Open", then click "Open".


Using it
--------

Drop your Excel files onto the left-hand panel (or click it to choose them),
then click SNUFF, SQUISH, or both. Your original files are never changed; the
repaired copy is saved right next to each one, with "(cleaned)" in its name.

The first time Snuffler saves into your Desktop, Documents or Downloads
folder, your Mac asks whether Snuffler may access that folder. Click "Allow".
